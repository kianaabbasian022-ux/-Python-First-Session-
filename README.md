Zayandeh Rood River - The Lifeline of Isfahan
## Introduction
Zayandeh Rood is one of the most important rivers in central Iran. Its name means "Life-Giving River" in Persian. The river originates in the Zagross Mountain and flows through Isfahan, Province. 
Historically, this river provides water for agriculture, industry and urban communities.
Several historical bridges cross the river which are well-known around the world. These bridges are important examples of Persian architecture and attract many visitors.
In recent decades, the river has experienced serious environmental challenges. Climate variability, drought, increasing agricultural demand, groundwater extraction, and water management decisions have contributed to water shortages. Unfortunately, nowadays the reduced flow has caused drought in the area and whole catchment. In addition, People facing some health and economical issues.
